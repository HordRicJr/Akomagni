A new way to work with AI

Akomagni is an open-source hybrid AI workstation. It brings models, agents, skills, memory, and workflows together so a simple request becomes a real work process.

Install now Browse tools

Quick install

Terminal 
curl -fsSL https://hordricjr.github.io/Akomagni/install/linux | bash
PowerShell 
irm https://hordricjr.github.io/Akomagni/install/windows | iex

Then connect once: akomagni connect (local, Rodium, Foundry, Hugging Face)

Intent to workflow, not just a chatbot

Skills give the model structured methods. Seventeen BMAD agents take specialized roles. Akomagni Flow routes your words to the right agent, skill, and workflow without slash commands.
